Hi, I am Haohao Zhu. I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Jiayu Zhou and a member of the Illidan Lab. My research interests include machine learning, causal reasoning, and multimodal AI. I am interested in building AI systems that are reliable, interpretable, and useful for real-world problems.

My current applications focus on healthcare and K–12 education. I explore how causal ideas and large language models help us understand text, images, and longitudinal records. In healthcare, I study clinical conversation generation, information-seeking triage, and conversational signals of cognitive impairment.

During my research internship at Ellipsis Health (May–August 2026), I worked on EHR-grounded conversation generation and LLM-based triage.
