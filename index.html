About

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Information at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Jiayu Zhou and a member of the Illidan Lab.

My research focuses on clinical conversation generation, causal reasoning, and multimodal AI, with applications in healthcare and K–12 education. I study how language models gather information through dialogue and make decisions grounded in clinical evidence, and how text, images, and longitudinal data can support reliable prediction.
